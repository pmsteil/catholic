3. **Censor 2 × optional citation item 12** (Newman "ch. 8, §2, and ch. 9"). The brief says to use censor fix 2's wording, so "ch. 8–9" stays. Not applied.

5. **Censor 7 × RA 55** (forgiveness). Remade on the censor's sentence. Only the full stop is added. "Grave sins", "1497; cf. 1452, 1458" and "the repentant sinner" are kept.
6. **Censor 10 × RA 70** (hell). The verb is moved forward, as RA asked. The censor's clause becomes its own sentence: "The Church teaches that He permits it out of respect for the freedom He gave (CCC 311, 1037); beyond that, she has not explained it." The substance is unchanged.
7. **Censor 13 × RA 28 × brief §2** (respect for persons). The censor's text names Kant. The brief's voice rule describes rivals without naming philosophers, so the name became a description: "the first great teacher of respect for persons held that reason must assume the soul lives on". "Today" is kept. RA 28 was then remade around it. If the main session wants the censor's exact words, restore "(Kant himself postulated the soul's immortality)".
8. **Censor 14 × RA 88** (Natural Law). RA 88's Old no longer matched. Remade with the censor's clause kept word for word: "what breaks love and how it is restored, though the judgment of each heart belongs to God (CCC 1861)". The "exactly … exactly" the censor removed was not brought back.
9. **Censor 17 × RA 8** (Sacrifice). RA 8 was remade on "(*Evangelium Vitae* 57, 65–66)".
10. **Citation 1 × RA 47** (VS citation). RA 47 was remade with "(cf. *Veritatis Splendor* 9, 40)".
11. **Citation 4 × report wording.** The citation report proposed "discern the signs of her spiritual soul". The brief requires the Catechism's own words, so the text reads: Reason can discern "signs of [the] spiritual soul" (CCC 33). CCC 366 is kept for the soul not perishing.
12. **Censor 11 × RA 76** ("inspired"). RA 76 dropped the word "inspired", but censor 11's new sentence ("though inspired") and the paragraph's last sentence still use it. The word is kept and glossed instead: "The Church holds those pages inspired: God is their author, working through their human authors, and they teach …" (DV 11).
13. **RA 75 accuracy** (private revelation). The item's gloss, "no message given to one person after Christ", is inexact, since private revelations can come to several people. Used "that is, no later message claimed from God" instead, which fits CCC 67. It uses commas, not a dash pair.
14. **RA 89** ("destiny" in CCC 1260) was not applied. The word is allowed inside the verbatim quotation, and the full magisterial quotation is kept (citation outranks rhythm).
15. **Brief numbering note.** The brief calls the DV 15 / VD 42 fix "censor fix 7". In censor.md it is finding 11; finding 7 is the CCC 1497 forgiveness fix. Both were applied in the censor's exact wording. The brief's list of cleared doctrinal read-aloud items ("55 … satisfaction gloss") is read as covering both RA 55 (full stop) and RA 56 (satisfaction gloss), and both were applied.
