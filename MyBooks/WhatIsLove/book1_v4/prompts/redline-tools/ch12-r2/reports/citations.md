## Fixes needed, most serious first

1. **VS 36 does not support "He is Himself the Good He asks of us".** §36 only describes the false-autonomy views the encyclical rejects; "the Good itself" is §9. Change `(cf. *Veritatis Splendor* 36, 40)` → `(cf. *Veritatis Splendor* 9, 40)`, and change the Further Study entry to match.
2. **Pieper's quotation does not match the edition the book cites.** The Ignatius 1997 edition reads "It's good that you exist" (pp. 163–64; only one secondary source). Quote that wording, give the page in Note 2, and drop "English renderings vary". Confirm it in Patrick's copy. Optionally reword "Love's first word" as "What love says".
3. **DI 10–13 supports only half the clause.** It shows how late dignity was seen, not how easily it is forgotten. Add ¶32 ("risks of regression"): `(cf. *Dignitas Infinita* 10–13, 32)`.
4. **CCC 33 is slightly overstated.** It says reason "discerns signs of his spiritual soul". Change to `Reason can discern the signs of her spiritual soul (CCC 33)`.
5. **Further Study is missing three sources cited in the body.** Add CCC 488, Gal 5:6, and the Summa articles II-II q.64 a.6 and q.171 a.5 (plus the ad 3 / ad 2 replies). Write Aquinas's dates as c. 1225.
6. **Two items still to check against a printed or direct source:**
   - Benedict XVI, *Jesus of Nazareth*, p. 74: only the fragment has been seen. Read the full sentence in the book.
   - *EV* 57 and 65: confirmed only through the IntraText concordance, because the fetch tool truncates the vatican.va page before §57. Read both sections once directly.
7. **Optional:**
   - "He takes away the whole guilt of the sin" has no citation.
   - The Eastern sayings (Zhu Xi's moon, "the sage abandons no one", "Heaven and Earth give life to things") have no source.
   - Newman could be cited as `ch. 8, §2, and ch. 9`.
