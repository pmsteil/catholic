# New rules for the book: review copy

Date: 2026-10-29. These are guidelines for writing new content and for double-checking important concepts. Nothing is in `memory.html` yet.

---

## A. Words and wording

**R1.** Never use the word "destiny". This ban covers the whole book.

**R2.** Avoid "progressive". Use "progression" or another word.

**R3.** In passages for general readers, avoid the loaded word "grace" and the phrase "cooperate with grace". Use "God's Perfect Love", "Jesus' Perfect Love", "the Lord's Perfect Love" or "His Perfect Love".

**R4.** Never write that God forces His gifts on us. Do not write "makes us righteous"; write "formed", "conformed" or "transformed". Jesus does not "master" us: we kneel and give ourselves to Him.

**R5.** Do not call a same-sex union a "marriage". Name the ideal "Complementary Covenant Marriage".

**R6.** Never say a source teaches more than it does. Check each claim of the form "exactly what the Church teaches".

**R7.** Virtue names are human words for larger truths. Never write that the book "defines" them. Offer the virtue matrix humbly, as leanings for the reader to consider, not as dogma.

## B. Voice and plain language

**R8.** Write plain English. Spell out "churchy" phrases and textbook definitions in concrete, everyday words, or define them in the sentence.

**R9.** Be concise. Add the smallest text that works. No "word salad", and nothing "wordy and heady".

**R10.** Do not dramatize. Prefer a plain statement to a flourish.

**R11.** Be humble when you offer a test or proof. Write "one way to test this is…", not an assertion.

**R12.** Do not end sections with a formula such as "if you have read this far". Vary the closing refrain.

**R13.** Do not re-argue covered ground. Assume the reader already accepts that faith alone is dead. Make no polemic against "faith alone"; make only the positive case that faith works through love.

## C. Concepts to get right

**R14.** Faith is an ascent/submission of the mind and will (or heart and soul or heart, mind and soul), never intellectual assent alone.

**R15.** Truth, justice and mercy are the core principles. Sacrifice makes them real. Sacrifice without the other three is not actuated love.

**R16.** Humility comes with or before faith. It is the submission of the whole self to God. True humility includes sacrifice.

**R17.** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. It is loving God and neighbor. It becomes concrete through named virtues, including hatred of sin. It is a lifelong journey, never instant.

**R18.** Cooperation with God is total gift, like spouses who each give everything.

**R19.** Atheists have the natural law written on their hearts. They can take part in God's truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice, but imperfectly and without justification. Atheism lacks the ability for true temporal or eternal communion. Communion needs united hearts, minds and souls and agreed truth.

**R20.** Never say God created us "for incorruption". God created us VERY GOOD.

**R21.** Men generally lean toward Justice and women toward Mercy. State as fact that sex is ordered to creation of children and unity of spouses. The same-sex counterfeit idolizes emotional bonding without a sacrificial desire for covenant.

## D. Working rules for Claude

**R22.** Show every proposed edit as a rendered redline: removed text in red strikethrough, added text in green. Use colored text only. Never use a white background or any fill. It must read well in dark mode. Show the full text. Wait for `y` or `n`. Never show a git diff.

**R23.** After you approve one redline, go straight to the next. Ask one issue at a time. "Skip" or "good as is" means leave it and move on.

**R24.** Give a link to every file or chapter you mention, so Patrick can open it. After multi-file edits, give one link per file.

**R25.** Move finished items from "In Progress" to "Completed" in `memory.html` without asking.

**R26.** When you report todos, print the exact todo text first, then your comment.

**R27.** Record why each decision was made, not only what was decided.

**R28.** Stay in scope. Log off-topic findings as a todo. Do not fix them. A kickoff prompt for a new session says "stay on topic", omits unrelated or already-flagged items, points to existing files instead of restating them, and carries no content from the previous chapter.

**R29.** Read-aloud review method. Read the whole chapter first. Rate each finding HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW or NOTE. Give the exact line, a quote, and a before and after. Never propose cutting load-bearing doctrine or Scripture for length. Write a styled HTML report in `chapters/reports/` with a summary table. Make no edits. Then walk through the findings one at a time.
