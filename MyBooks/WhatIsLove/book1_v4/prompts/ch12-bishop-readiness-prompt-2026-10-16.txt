STANDING RULES
- The canonical definition, word for word: "God's Perfect Love is the sacred gift of covenant which binds truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice into life-giving communion." List form "truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice" (no Oxford comma).
- Facets, one image only: love is one diamond seen from every side. Truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice are facets too, never a separate class. Never "pillars" or "threads"; avoid "the four facets" (name the four, or say "the facets of love"). Where a sentence sums up what love is, name every facet. Don't stretch the diamond image.
- Chapter references say what the chapter was about ("Chapter 5, on how love binds"), not just the number (Future Task 178).
- Banned words: performed/performance, earned/unearned, transaction, payment plan, debt-payment, checklist, unmercy, destiny, imputed/imputation. No debt/payment framing of the Cross; prefer Christ's self-offering in love.
- Say "the marital act", "St. John Paul II", "Pope St. Paul VI", "Our Lady", "the Sacrament of Reconciliation" (everyday "go to Confession" is fine). Sacrifice and self-gift are interchangeable. Give every thinker their proper title (todo 127).
- *Author* is reserved for God. Grace first; no new open/closed/empty-hand imagery (todo 129). Only God is the summum bonum. The Church on earth is never "perfect". No "what Jesus really meant" framing.
- Catholics are not "people of the book": "the Christian faith is not a 'religion of the book'" (CCC 108). Scripture is God's word of love, given to be lived.
- No polemic against Protestants; no Protestant thinkers as authority. No Greek or Hebrew script. Plain words; define churchy terms at first use. No metaphor that hides the meaning. Don't start sentences with "So." Don't overclaim. Examples must be everyday and fit the claim. Counterfeits are counterfeits of love, never people.
- Footnotes are raw <sup>N</sup> plus a "### Notes" list. Ch. 12 has none now; Ch. 11 kept none. Tell me if Ch. 12 needs them before adding any.
- Don't reopen rejected items (memory.html §14) or settled items in §3 and §11.
