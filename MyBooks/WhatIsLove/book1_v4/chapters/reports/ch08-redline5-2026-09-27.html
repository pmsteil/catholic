1 · Empathy: good, but not the same as love (Binding Check)

Section: Binding Check · line 267

Mercy is the pillar most often mistaken for the whole of love — which means its distortions are the most common, and the hardest to see. In our day the mistake usually goes by the name empathy. Empathy, feeling with another, is a good thing; Pope Leo XIV calls compassion and empathy "a call from God to reflect his goodness in our daily lives."2 But feeling with someone is not yet willing their good. Bishop Robert Barron, following St. Thomas, puts it plainly: "Love is an act of the will, not a feeling of the heart."3 Set in the place of love and cut loose from truth and justice, empathy cannot tell comfort from cure.
