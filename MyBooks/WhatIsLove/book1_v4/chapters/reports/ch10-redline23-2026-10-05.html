Eucharistic Prayer III is gone, as you asked. Only about six of the world’s roughly 195 nations have no public Catholic church to walk into: Saudi Arabia, the Maldives, Afghanistan, Somalia, Bhutan and North Korea. In North Korea the one Catholic church is state-run, with no priest in communion with Rome. Even in those countries Catholics gather to pray in private where they can. So “nearly every nation on earth” is true and shows the reach. “Every time zone” is dropped because we can’t check it.
