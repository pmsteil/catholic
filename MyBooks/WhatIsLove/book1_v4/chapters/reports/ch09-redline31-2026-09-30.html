The boast in its proper place.

Faith Working Through Love: boast of the Lord

The old ending made the joy something we own. Now the joy is His, given: “He fills the open hand with His own joy,” echoing John 15:11, “that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be full.” That keeps the boast and the joy both pointing to Him.

Section: Faith Working Through Love · line 187

So nobody gets to boast. There is nothing to boast about. An open hand has not produced the gift placed in it; it has simply stopped refusing it. We began to trust because He is good, and we keep trusting and obeying for the same reason: He is good. Every act of love we manage is His love borrowed for a moment, set down in a hand that finally consented to be open. The clenching was ours. The gift is His. When we love, we are not adding anything to God; we are saying with our lives what we pray with our lips: Thy will be done. The boast belongs to Him. The joy of the open hand belongs to us So we boast, gladly, of Him: "therefore, as it is written, 'Let him who boasts, boast of the Lord.'" (1 Corinthians 1:31). We have an all-loving God to boast about, and He fills the open hand with His own joy (cf. John 15:11).
