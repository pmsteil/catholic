Partiality: justice lived one soul at a time

line 173

… the God in whose image every person was made. ¶In the end, justice is owed, received and lived one soul at a time. A group has no face and no heart of its own; only a person does: "the human person . . . is and ought to be the principle, the subject and the end of all social institutions" (CCC 1881). Unjust laws must still be changed (cf. CCC 1888), but no law has ever converted a heart. That happens one person at a time, as each heart, moved by grace, freely submits to God's justice.
