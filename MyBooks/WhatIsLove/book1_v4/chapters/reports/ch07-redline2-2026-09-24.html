Gratitude: the debt we can never repay (Psalm 116)

line 117

… This is why justice toward God can never be settled. St. Thomas observes that It is a debt of gratitude we can never repay, and the psalmist knew it:¶ "What shall I render to the LORD for all his bounty to me? I will lift up the cup of salvation and call on the name of the LORD, I will pay my vows to the LORD in the presence of all his people."¶— Psalm 116:12-14 ¶The psalmist asks how to repay what can never be repaid, and every answer he gives is worship: he lifts the cup, he calls on the name of the Lord, he keeps his vows before all the people. St. Thomas reaches for this same psalm to explain why religion belongs to justice and yet can never make equal return: "whatever man renders to God is due, yet it cannot be equal, as though man rendered to God as much as he owes Him" (ST II-II, q.80, a.1). …
