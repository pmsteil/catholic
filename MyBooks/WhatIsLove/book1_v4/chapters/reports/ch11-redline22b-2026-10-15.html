Redline 22 · God is the Author; a faith lived, not a book

Section: The Work of a Lifetime · line 276

This chapter opens the door — but the comprehensive treatment cannot be written here, because it is not mine to write. That book is yours: the one you will write with your life Nor is it yours. God is the Author, and the Church says plainly that "the Christian faith is not a 'religion of the book'"; it is the religion of the Word of God, "not a written and mute word, but incarnate and living" (CCC 108). Scripture is God's word of love to us, and it is given to be lived: to love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul and mind, and your neighbor as yourself (cf. Matthew 22:37–39). Your part is to live it, with a heart and mind formed by His love, in the daily, costly, faithful practice of His perfect love. Practiced faithfully, it is the book that saves — you, and your household, and through your household, the world — that love. And that is how evil is overcome: not by your own strength, but by His love alive in you, overcoming evil with good (cf. Romans 12:21) — first in your own heart, then in your household, and through your household, in the world.
