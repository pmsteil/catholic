Father and mother, moved from Chapter 10

Section: The Prodigal Son: The Icon of How to Love · line 101

… which son am I looking at — and will I be the father, or one of the counterfeits? ¶Most families know these two sons from the inside. A father often leans toward justice, and his temptation is to become the elder brother; a mother often leans toward mercy, and hers is to throw the party in the far country. This is part of how God made us. The Church teaches that the "difference and complementarity" of man and woman "are oriented toward the goods of marriage and the flourishing of family life" (CCC 2333). Each needs the other. His justice without her mercy crushes a child; her mercy without his justice spoils one. Held together, they begin to look like the father on the road, for in our Father in heaven, justice and mercy are one.
