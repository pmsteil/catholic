Redline 15 · the Church's teaching under the three movements

Section: The Shape of a Wise Decision · line 128

… Prudence, St. Thomas Aquinas teaches, completes itself in three movements (Summa Theologiae II-II, q.47, a.8) — and in each of them, truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice are doing the quiet work. That is no accident. As we saw in Chapter 5, on how love binds every virtue together, the Church calls charity "the form of the virtues" (CCC 1827). Prudence is not a second rule standing beside love; it is love doing its thinking.
