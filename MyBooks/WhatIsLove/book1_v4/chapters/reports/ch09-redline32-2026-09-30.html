The Disciple at the Teacher's Feet: the pillar each counterfeit is missing

I checked each item against the pillars and the standing rule (“hypocrisy is truth-without-sacrifice”). Three change.
Lips without the heart (Matt 15:8): words without the heart is hypocrisy, but the heart held back from God is also the self-gift withheld, so “truth and sacrifice.”
Outside of the cup (Matt 23:25–28): the inside is “full of extortion and rapacity,” which is theft, so justice belongs with truth: “truth and justice.”
Heavy burdens (Matt 23:4): binding others to what they won't carry themselves is a double standard, which is justice. “Will not lift a finger” is the plainest refusal of cost in the list, which is sacrifice. So “justice and sacrifice” replaces “justice and mercy.”
Unchanged, because they're right: piety for show (truth and sacrifice); the Sabbath healing (mercy: “to do good or to do harm, to save life or to kill?”); the woman caught in adultery (mercy, and justice in the absent man).
