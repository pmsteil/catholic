2 · Acts 20:28: Paul hands the flock to the overseers

line 216

… They wrote letters to correct doctrine, meant to be kept and read after they were gone (2 Pet 1:15 — "And I will see to it that after my departure you may be able at any time to recall these things"). They charged those they left behind to govern: taking leave of the elders of Ephesus, knowing they would never see him again (Acts 20:25), Paul told them, "Take heed to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to care for the church of God which he obtained with the blood of his own Son" (Acts 20:28) — and the Greek word for overseers is episkopoi, the word that would become bishops. And from the very start they replaced Judas with Matthias (Acts 1:20–26) — establishing that the apostolic office could outlive the man who held it. …
