Attacks on permanence. Divorce culture treats "I'm not happy" as sufficient reason to break covenant; , and no-fault divorce wrote that idea into law: a marriage can now be ended at the wish of one spouse, with no wrong to name and no cause to show. The law that once guarded the promise now guards the exit. The Church calls divorce what it is: "a grave offense against the natural law," because "it claims to break the contract, to which the spouses freely consented, to live with each other till death" (CCC 2384). Meanwhile, hookup culture and pornography use other bodies for pleasure without giving self.
