Section: Truth and Authority › Christ Shares the Author's Authority with His Church · line 291

The Church's authority, then, serves not truth alone but the integration of truth with justice, mercy and sacrifice — the whole of God's love bound together. Her authority is itself an expression of that love (as we saw in Chapter 5, and as Chapter 10 will reveal fully in the Eucharist).

Christ, the Author made flesh, shared His authority with the Church that He said He would build (Matthew 16:18); to "start my own church," even out of love for Him, is to claim an authority He never gave anyone and to take up the one work He kept for Himself.
