“Lent to your hands” makes grace sound like a loan held at arm’s length. Scripture’s own words are better: “God’s love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit” (Romans 5:5). “Written on your heart” echoes Jeremiah 31:33, but there it is God’s law that is written; for His love and life, “poured into” is the Bible’s word, and it says what “enabled in your soul” was reaching for: the Holy Spirit Himself living in us. Cited with “cf.” since only part of the verse is used; RSVCE and RSV agree on the clause.
