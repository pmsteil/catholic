Section: How Sacrifice Combats Evil · line 305

… Each deadly sin is ultimately a refusal of God's perfect love itself, and because that love is one undivided thing, every sin refuses truth, justice, mercy, and sacrifice all at once. Beneath each sin lies the same prior posture: the soul curved inward, the closed fist that will not look outside itself to receive what is given. Sacrifice is the most visible face of that closure — because hands that will not open are easy to see — and so it is the face this chapter has been asking us to look at most carefully.
