Section: What Mercy Actually Does: The Works of Mercy · line 253

… Two thousand years of Catholic hospitals, orphanages, food kitchens, schools, prison ministries, hospices, missions, and burial societies are nothing other than the Church doing these fourteen things — on a scale and across a span of history no other institution has matched. Today, by the Holy See's own count, the Church runs more than 5,300 hospitals, nearly 14,000 clinics, over 15,500 homes for the elderly, the chronically ill and the disabled, and more than 8,500 orphanages.2 Mercy is not what we feel about the suffering; it is what we do for them.
