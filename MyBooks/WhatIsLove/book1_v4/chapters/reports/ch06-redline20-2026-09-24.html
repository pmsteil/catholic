Faith in Christ is submission to Him as Teacher

line 281

The Church has measured herself instead by what Irenaeus, writing around the year 180, called the rule of truth — the faith received from the apostles, which she "guards with care, as dwelling in but a single house, and similarly believes as if having but one soul and a single heart" (CCC 173). A divided Christianity preaches a divisible Christ; a divisible Christ preaches a divisible truth; and a divisible truth is precisely the lie the evil one and the world most wants to believe. ¶Submitting to the one Church Christ founded is not tribalism and it is not mere preference — it is obedience to the prayer of Jesus, and it is how the world is drawn toward its salvation. Nor is this something added on to faith in Christ; it belongs to faith itself. To put our trust in Christ is to receive Him not only as Savior but as Teacher: "You call me Teacher and Lord; and you are right, for so I am" (John 13:13). Faith, the Church teaches, is the free submission of our mind and will to God who reveals, trusting what He says "because its truth is guaranteed by God, who is Truth itself" (CCC 143–144). And the Teacher chose how He would go on teaching: through the apostles He sent, and through the living Church He built on them (cf. Luke 10:16). …
