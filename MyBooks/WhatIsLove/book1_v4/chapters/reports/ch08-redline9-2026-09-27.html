St. John Paul II had seen what grows in hearts where mercy is refused: a grievance nursed until it becomes hatred, a neighbor renamed an enemy, whole peoples declared beyond redemption, until the hatred is organized into camps and rail lines. Mercy is the one force that works at that root. If we who have received it took it seriously, living it in our homes and carrying it to the whole world, it would overcome evil with good (cf. Romans 12:21) before the evil could grow: one forgiven heart, then another, each refusing to hand the hatred on. That is the Gospel sharpened to a single point for a generation that had nearly forgotten it: no sin is greater than His mercy, and the door is open right now.
