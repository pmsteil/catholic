What the Mass is and does, in the Catechism's words

Transformed, in the Catechism's terms: the Mass “completes and surpasses all the sacrifices of the Old Covenant” (CCC 1330), so Israel's offerings are fulfilled, not discarded. Applied to us in time: the Eucharist “re-presents (makes present) the sacrifice of the cross” and “applies its fruit” (CCC 1366). Both read on vatican.va. “In our own time and place” is our gloss on “applies,” and sits outside the quotation marks.

In Christ, Israel's offerings are not left behind but fulfilled: the Mass "completes and surpasses all the sacrifices of the Old Covenant" (CCC 1330), because it "re-presents (makes present) the sacrifice of the cross" and "applies its fruit" to us, in our own time and place (CCC 1366).
