CCC 82, second sentence

… not a book waiting to be compiled, but the Church, "in her doctrine, life and worship," transmitting "to every generation all that she herself is, all that she believes" (CCC 78). Scripture is the Word of God set down in writing, and the Church reveres it above every other text; but she "does not derive her certainty about all revealed truths from the holy Scriptures alone. Both Scripture and Tradition must be accepted and honored with equal sentiments of devotion and reverence" (CCC 82).
