The Day of Atonement paragraph.

Hebrews 9: once for all, and what the Mass gives us

The reference names where both sentences come from (Heb 9:11 and 9:24, “made with hands … heaven itself”; 9:25–26, “yearly … once for all”). Then the Church's own words for what the Mass does for us: CCC 1364, quoting Vatican II, “the work of our redemption is carried out”; and CCC 1382, “to receive communion is to receive Christ himself who has offered himself for us.” Both read in full on vatican.va. The full teaching (CCC 1366–1367, Trent) stays in “Why We Have Mass.”

Section: The Biblical Foundation: From Shadow to Reality · line 83

… Not an earthly tabernacle made by hands, but heaven itself. Not yearly, but once, for all time (cf. Hebrews 9:11, 24–26). That one offering is what every Mass makes present and gives to us: in it "the work of our redemption is carried out," and "to receive communion is to receive Christ himself who has offered himself for us" (CCC 1364, 1382).
