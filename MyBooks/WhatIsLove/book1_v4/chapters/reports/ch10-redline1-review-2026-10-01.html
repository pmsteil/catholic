    1 · The opening picks up Chapter 9's Bridge; communion is God's gift (item 6, grace first)

    Unity Requires Submission to the Authority of His Perfect Love · line 300

    Notice what this means: these are not four separate tests but a single one, because the four threads pillars are facets of one undivided Love. Remove any one and the others unravel give way with it—truth without mercy expels, mercy without truth enables, justice without sacrifice finds no one willing to pay bear its cost, sacrifice without justice is merely exploited. Real communion requires all four working simultaneously, in the same act—the way a single beam of white light requires every color at once, and shatters into something lesser the instant one is filtered out. This is why only God's Perfect Love can create a unity that lasts: in Him truth, justice, mercy and sacrifice are not four God, truth, justice and mercy are not ingredients held in careful balance but one seamless reality with His very being, and on the Cross that one love gave itself whole, holding nothing back. The world keeps trying to build communion while cutting out the very threads that make it possible knocking out the very pillars that hold it up—and then wonders why its movements curdle into mobs, its marriages into contracts, and its nations into warring tribes.

    Unity is not something we build or create or force upon others; it is what happens when we submit together to the Authority of His Perfect Love. Every counterfeit unity—coerced, comfortable, or merely agreeable—lacks one of love's four threads pillars, and so it cannot last.
