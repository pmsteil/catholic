Transformed into what we receive

Section: How Love Creates Life-Giving Communion › The Mass Is How the Church Reads Her Bible · line 249

… We do not invent the Christian life; we walk into a rhythm Christ Himself authored and His Church has carried unbroken across the ages. To go to Mass is to step into the place where all four pillars of God's Perfect Love are held together in a single hour, week after week, until they remake us into His likeness. The Second Vatican Council, quoting Pope St. Leo the Great, puts it plainly: "the partaking of the body and blood of Christ does nothing other than make us be transformed into that which we consume" (Lumen Gentium 26). Fed at the table of His Word and His Body, we become less of our old selves and more of Him: "He must increase, but I must decrease" (John 3:30).
