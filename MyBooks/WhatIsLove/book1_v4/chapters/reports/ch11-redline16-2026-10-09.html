The Catechism gives this sacrament five names, not three: conversion, Penance, confession, forgiveness and Reconciliation (CCC 1423–1424, under the heading “The Sacrament of Penance and Reconciliation”). The rest of the book already uses “Sacrament of Reconciliation” (Chapters 7, 8 and 14), and Chapter 14 adds “(Confession)” once. Chapter 11 is the only place that calls it the “Sacrament of Confession”. These two edits bring it into line, and keep the familiar word in brackets the first time.
