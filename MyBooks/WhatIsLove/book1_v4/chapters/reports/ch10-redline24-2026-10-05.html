The Our Father. Mercy: "forgive Lord's Prayer. The prayer Christ taught us asks, line by line, for the communion we are about to receive. "Our Father": we never pray it alone, but as children of one Father, and "if we pray the Our Father sincerely, we leave individualism behind, because the love that we receive frees us from it" (CCC 2792). "Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven": we ask that His will, not ours, rule our lives here as it rules in heaven. "Give us this day our daily bread": the Church teaches that the word "daily," taken literally, "refers directly to the Bread of Life, the Body of Christ, the 'medicine of immortality,' without which we have no life within us" (CCC 2837), the very words of St. Ignatius. "Forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us." "Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil": we ask Him to guard the communion He gives against everything that would break it. And the Church prays it at the very threshold of Communion, where, she tells us, it "knocks at the door of the Banquet of the kingdom which sacramental communion anticipates" (CCC 2770).
