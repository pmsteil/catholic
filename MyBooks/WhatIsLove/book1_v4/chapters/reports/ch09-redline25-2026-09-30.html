The Open Hand: receiving comes first

Your instinct is right and matches the grace-first rule: as written, “the hand opens by giving” could read as if we open it ourselves. A short paragraph now comes first. It walks the four pillars as received (truth about who we are, justice, mercy asked for, His life), anchors it in Romans 5:8, and ends “Only a hand that has received can give.” Then “opens by giving” becomes “stays open by giving,” so the existing paragraph describes growth. One correction: we don't deserve forgiveness. Justice tells us we deserve judgment, and shows that guilt carried on the Cross; mercy is exactly what we don't deserve. So the justice clause is worded that way. Romans 5:8 matches in the RSVCE and RSV.
