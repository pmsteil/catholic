# Censor report: the opening "Form" paragraph and section A (A1 to A7)

## Scope
**Read:** all of ARGUMENT.md for context. I examined every sentence and citation of the "**Form:**" paragraph (line 3), the A preamble (line 5) and A1 to A7 (lines 6 to 12). I also checked passages elsewhere only where my part depends on them: W1(2) on 1 Samuel 15, the C section on Nietzsche, and the Conclusion.

**Checked against:** the CCC (English, vatican.va); *Dignitas Infinita*, *Samaritanus Bonus* and *Veritatis Splendor* (vatican.va); *Evangelium Vitae* (vatican.va, IntraText, University of Navarra); *Dei Filius* ch. 2 to 4 and can. 2.1 and 3.6; Trent Sess. XIV; ST I-II q.94 a.2, a.4, a.6 and q.100 a.8 (New Advent); the RSVCE; Legge's *Mencius*; and standard sources for Seneca and the Vinaya.

**Fetch limits:** the vatican.va pages for EV and VS were cut off by the fetch tool after EV 48 and VS 56. EV 57 and 65 to 66 and VS 80 were therefore checked elsewhere (see section 2).

## Bottom line
No Errors.

My part contains nothing contrary to faith or morals. There are 14 Needs care items. The most important are these four:
- (F9) A4's wording "the line binds us and not the Lord of life". It can be read as voluntarism, or as God dispensing the Decalogue, which St. Thomas expressly denies.
- (F11) The Church's definitive moral teaching is labelled "this book's claims", in A4 and again in A6.
- (F13) A7's "follow the evidence ... binds believer and unbeliever alike" needs a safeguard drawn from *Dei Filius* ch. 3.
- (F2) CCC 31 is cited for "not a proof that compels him". That shifts the paragraph's meaning towards underclaiming.

## 3 Verdict
**As it stands:** my part contains nothing contrary to faith or morals, so a *nihil obstat* is not barred. As the strictest kind of censor, though, I would not sign until four items are corrected, because each leaves a reading a hostile or careless reader could fairly take as erroneous:
- F9: the Decalogue appearing dispensable by God.
- F11: Church teaching labelled as "this book's claims".
- F13: the believer's faith appearing to be held in suspense pending evidence.
- F2: CCC 31 used to underclaim reason's reach.

**With the fixes:** yes. With F1–F23 applied (or at least F2, F6, F9, F10, F11, F13, F14 and F15), I would grant the *nihil obstat* to the Form paragraph and section A without reservation. F25 belongs to the censor of section C.
