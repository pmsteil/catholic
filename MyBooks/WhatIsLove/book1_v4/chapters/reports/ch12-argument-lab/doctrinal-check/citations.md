**Wrong number (1)**

1. **Three things / *Second*, "the lion lives by the slaying of other animals (ST I q.22 a.2 ad 2; q.49 a.2)".** I q.22 a.2 ad 2 is right ("A lion would cease to live, if there were no slaying of animals"). I q.49 a.2 does not mention the lion. It says God causes the corruption of things "by accident" in willing the order of the universe. The lion-and-ass text is **I q.48 a.2 ad 3**: "nor would the life of a lion be preserved unless the ass were killed." Fix: "(ST I q.22 a.2 ad 2; q.48 a.2 ad 3; cf. q.49 a.2)".

**Citation does not carry the whole clause it is attached to (1)**

2. **Evil / hell, "she has never declared of any human being that he is lost, for without a special revelation no one can know who is (John Paul II, General Audience, 28 July 1999, 4)".** §4 says: "Damnation remains a real possibility, but it is not granted to us, without special divine revelation, to know which human beings are effectively involved in it." It does not say that the Church has never declared anyone lost. That clause is true as history, but it is not this source's statement. Fix: "she does not declare of any human being that he is lost, for without special divine revelation it is not granted to us to know which human beings are (John Paul II, General Audience, 28 July 1999, 4)".

**Partial support: add or adjust a reference (5)**

3. **W1(2), "a call to communion with Him … in which the Church finds the root reason for human dignity (GS 19; CCC 1700)".** GS 19 says it exactly. CCC 1700 roots dignity "in his creation in the image and likeness of God," which is a different point. The CCC paragraph that matches is **CCC 27**: "The dignity of man rests above all on the fact that he is called to communion with God." Fix: "(GS 19; CCC 27)".
4. **W1(2), "every child was wanted by the One who made her, before anyone saw her (Jeremiah 1:5)".** The verse is about Jeremiah's own call. The Church applies it to every human life in **CCC 2270**, which cites Jer 1:5. Fix: "(cf. Jeremiah 1:5; CCC 2270)".
5. **Evil, "God kept Mary from every sin by grace without forcing her yes (CCC 491–493)".** 491–493 support "from every sin by grace." Nothing in them speaks to her free yes. CCC 488 ("he wanted the free co-operation of a creature") and 494 ("giving her consent to God's word") do. Fix: "(CCC 488, 491–494)".
6. **C, The Way, "the cruelty of rulers, the newborn as the image of whole virtue, a sage who abandons no one … (A1, A4; *Daodejing* 27, 49, 67)".** 27, 49 and 67 cover the sage who casts away no one, goodness to the good and the not-good, and the three treasures. The newborn is **ch. 55** ("like an infant"). The rulers who kill, and "to kill in the Executioner's place is to cut in the master carpenter's place," are **ch. 74**, which the text uses but does not cite. Fix: "(A1, A4; *Daodejing* 27, 49, 55, 67, 74)", or cite ch. 74 after the executioner sentence.
7. **W1(2) and Evil, "(so too Aquinas, *Summa contra Gentiles* III.112)" for God willing each person for her own sake.** III.112 says intellectual creatures are governed "for their own sake." That each one is directed as an individual and not only as a member of the species is the subject of **III.113** ("not only to the species, but also to the individual"). Fix: "SCG III.112–113" in both places.

**Minor precision (2; optional)**

8. **W1(2), "read in the light of Christ … (*Dei Verbum* 15; *Verbum Domini* 42)".** VD 42 has the "violence and massacre, without explicitly denouncing" text. The phrase "read the Old Testament in the light of Christ crucified and risen" is in **VD 41**. Fix: "*Verbum Domini* 41–42".
9. **C, Impersonal sacred order, "*Analects* 7.23, 14.35".** Both passages exist. These numbers follow later editions such as D.C. Lau's. Legge numbers them VII.22 and XIV.37. Fix: name the translation in the bibliography.
