# Whole-text censor's report: ARGUMENT.md (Chapter 12 moral argument)

## Scope

**What I read.** I read CENSOR_BRIEF.md and the whole of ARGUMENT.md, twice: the Form paragraph, A (A1–A7), W1 (with W1(1), W1(2) and the "This Lawgiver" paragraph), W2, W3, every C row, the section on evil (including its "Three things" paragraph) and the Conclusion. I opened nothing else under /tmp/claude-0/.

**What I checked.** Other censors are checking each section sentence by sentence. I checked only what shows across the whole text:
- contradictions between sections;
- the overall doctrinal shape, and what the text's own claims require but leave out;
- how much authority each claim is given;
- tone and pastoral fitness;
- whether the book's three stated positions (on 1 Samuel 15, the conclusion as a weighing, and hell as the heaviest weight) are worded so that a censor could sign them.

**Sources used.** On vatican.va this session I checked *Spe Salvi*, *Veritatis Splendor*, *Evangelium Vitae* 34, and CCC 1033–1037 and 1440–1460. I checked ST I-II q.100 a.8 on newadvent.org. For the remaining texts I used Scripture (RSVCE), the CCC, *Dei Filius*, Trent and St. Thomas as I know them, and section 2 says which ones these are.

## Bottom line

No Errors.

Nothing in the whole text is contrary to faith or morals. Several things show only when the sections are read together, and they should be corrected before print. The most important are F1, F3, F5 and F9, all about how the sections fit together.
- **F1.** The 1 Samuel 15 position is not tied to the absoluteness of the negative precept (VS 52; EV 57).
- **F3.** "God alone forgives" sits beside "not of God alone", and nothing says that forgiveness comes through the Cross.
- **F5.** Church teaching is labelled "this book's claims".
- **F9.** Hell is called a "strain" on the principle, and the principle is held "in hope".

## 3 Verdict

**As it stands:** no Error. Read as one work, the text presents Catholic teaching faithfully. Because of the Needs-care items found across sections, especially F1, F3, F5 and F9, I would not yet sign it as it stands.

**With the fixes:** yes. With F1–F16 applied I would grant the *nihil obstat* to the whole.
