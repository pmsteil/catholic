## 1 Bottom line

**Errors: one.**
- **Row 1, C, "Reason's own law" (Kant).** The text says Kant "held that not even God may use a rational being merely as a means," and then says the book "parts from him about God". A careful reader can take that to mean the book holds that God may use a person merely as a means. That reading is contrary to GS 24, and to the book's own W1(2) and its section on evil. The fix says the book agrees with Kant on that point. It also moves the disagreement to where it really lies: whether a death God commands is still the killing the precept forbids.

**Nihil obstat:** yes, with the fixes made. All six checkers grant it on that condition, and I agree.
- **What a censor would require before signing:** Row 1, and the convergent doctrinal items in Rows 2–10.
- **What should be settled before print:** the open items on the Verify list, especially items 1, 3 and 4. They are *Evangelium Vitae* 57–66 and *Veritatis Splendor* 80 on vatican.va or in print, and the Denzinger number for the craniotomy responses.
