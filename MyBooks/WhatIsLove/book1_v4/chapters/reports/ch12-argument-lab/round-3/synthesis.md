# Round 3 synthesis — v3 against 11 schools + doctrinal censor (2026-10-18)

**Scores, v3 as written (repaired):** Realist 7.5 (8.5) · Debunker 8 (9) · Aristotelian 8 (9) · Kantian 8.5 (9) · Contractualist 7.5 (8.5) · Utilitarian 8 (9) · Expressivist 8 (9) · Nietzschean 8.5 (9) · Buddhist 7.5 (9) · Confucian 8 (9) · Evil 8 (8.5). **Mean 7.95** (R1 5.6 → R2 6.6 → R3 7.95). **No Fatal objection from any school.**

**Stop test ("As written: NONE"):** met by **3 of 11** — Kantian, Nietzschean, and the problem-of-evil atheist (the school Patrick most wanted on the panel). The other 8 each logged one or two Serious objections, every one answerable at parity by exact wording.

## The consensus fault: W1(1)'s either/or (6 schools)
Debunker, Realist, Aristotelian, Expressivist, Confucian, Utilitarian (and Contractualist in its repairs). "Either the debt died with her ... or she is still someone" leaves out the **third road**: the debt outlives her, owed to her *as she was*, and can never be received, released or forgiven. That is the tragic view (Gaita, Williams, Feinberg, Pitcher) and the ancestral road of repaying the dead (*bao*; Confucius sacrificed "as though they were present," *Analects* 3.12; Xunzi ch. 19). v3 called it "only remorse," which is unfair, and since A3 is a shared premise, the two-horned version in effect deduced survival from common ground and then called it a clue. **Repair:** name all three roads; honor the second; rest the clue on what the second cannot do — what the living pay, no one receives; what cannot be repaid, no one releases; the kinless dead get nothing; guilt asks *to be forgiven by her*, which only the third road can give. Its weight is as much as the reader finds the third truer than the second to the guilt he actually knows.

## The W1 test is too coarse (Kantian, Realist, Contractualist, Expressivist, Aristotelian, Buddhist, Confucian)
"Owed or only ripening?" separates theism from karma and bare necessary truths, not from second-personal accounts: while she lives, every serious account says guilt is owed to her. **Repair:** "We agree she is owed. We part at the grave, and over what gives her the claim at all."

## Other Serious items (one school each)
- **Contractualist:** C row misdescribed Scanlon ("owed only what someone agrees") and contradicted its own "born one of us." Rewritten from the school's own statement.
- **Utilitarian:** (1) "No one is the price of someone else's good, not even in God's hands" reads as denying the Cross (Mark 10:45; 1 Cor 6:20; Col 1:24; *Salvifici Doloris* 19) — also flagged by the censor and the Aristotelian. (2) A6's exceptionless absolute was presented as shared data; at catastrophe scale assent fails (threshold deontology, Walzer, Hiroshima). **Repair:** the Church holds the line at every number (VS 80); that is the book's claim, argued in W1(2), not a shared one.
- **Aristotelian:** W1(2) overclaimed: Foot and Thompson explain "may not kill one for five" without God (A6's magistrate case is Foot's own). The Church's real ground was missing: **life is held in trust from the Lord of life (CCC 2258, 2280)** — jurisdiction, not arithmetic — which also explains why the line has no exceptions and is not even her own to cross (where Foot parted from the Church).
- **Realist:** W2 dropped the third-factor reply and C said realists cannot say where necessity lives (they say: in the essences of things). **Repair:** credit both; press *reach* (how conscience sees what holds for any mind in any world; the parallel with mathematics); "It says the law holds of itself. We say it holds first in a Mind."
- **Buddhist:** the Dhamma row's "answers suffering by desert ... costs A4" misstates (the answer is the four truths) and the cost does not follow (compassion does not track karma); the evil section omitted the rival's real strength — it never said that it is good that we exist, so it bears natural evil more easily. **Repair:** credit it; name the real divide (the goodness of being; the highest good by insight vs by gift, AN 8.29 vs CCC 1250, 1261).
- **Confucian:** the third road (above); minor: Xunzi conflated with Zhu Xi; "gives life to kinds" → "to each thing, and intends none"; Mou is not "impersonal."

## The doctrinal censor (pre-imprimatur read) — 5 Errors, all to be fixed in v4
1. **The ashes example.** The Church does not permit scattering ashes at sea (*Ad resurgendum cum Christo* 7) — the example treated it as the honored promise. **Retired.** Replaced by a betrayed secret (with the utilitarian's storm-style contrast).
2. **A4 "the enemy ... never to be killed"** contradicts legitimate defense (CCC 2263–2265). → "never, if innocent, to be deliberately killed" (CCC 2261), dignity not lost even by grave crimes (CCC 2267).
3. **A6 "experimented on to death *without consent*"** implies consent could justify it (CCC 2295). → "not even his own consent could make it right."
4. **W1(1)** collided with CCC 1441 ("Only God forgives sins"), made God's forgiveness partial ("that part"), omitted the Church and the Sacrament (CCC 1442), and read as soul-sleep ("so that she is there") against CCC 366, 1023. → God's forgiveness is whole; absolution "does not remedy all the disorders sin has caused" (CCC 1459), so amends are owed and the pardon that heals the bond between two people remains hers to give; the soul does not perish; all the dead will rise (CCC 998); the communion of saints and *Spe Salvi* 48 ("a request for pardon" to the dead is "never in vain"); the Church does not spell out how the last reconciliation is completed.
5. **"No one is the price"** — see the Utilitarian item. → "God never spends anyone as a mere means against her own good ... what Christ gave was given, not taken."

**Needs care (selected):** A5's list must match ST I-II q.94 a.2 (life; man and woman and children; truth, including about God; society); disordered desires are not God's work (CCC 405); "wrong first because of what it does to the child" vs CCC 1440 ("before all else an offense against God"); "the wrong is necessary" → "its wrongness is necessary"; evolution and the soul (CCC 366; *Humani Generis* 36); desire is healed, not killed (*Deus Caritas Est* 5); NA 2 must not be cut before "proclaims Christ"; CCC 36 and *Dei Filius* (God can be known with certainty) beside CCC 37; CCC 29 (one can hide from God); hiddenness "at the end" must not suggest a chance after death (CCC 1021; GS 22); "a God who suffers" → the Son suffered in His flesh; *impassibilis sed non incompassibilis* (*Spe Salvi* 39); the resurrection is professed, not only hoped (CCC 998); hell is real and eternal (CCC 1035); God "a stopping point" must not read as brute (Ex 3:14; ST I q.3 a.4); CCC 31's full sentence ("allow us to attain certainty").

- "It says the law holds of itself. We say it holds first in a Mind."
- "Drain one river, and the moon has lost nothing. God does not lose her; He keeps her."

- "A yes said to everything is said to no one."
