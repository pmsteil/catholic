## The Error (censor) — must fix before anything else
**Kant row:** "He did not make that Ruler the author of the law, and neither does this book (Chapter 3)." Read plainly, it denies that God is the author of the moral law. *Veritatis Splendor* 36, 40 (verified on vatican.va): the natural law "has God as its author." It also contradicts the chapter title, the W1 closing, W2 and the conclusion. Intended meaning: God is its author, not as a will that invents it; the law is His own wisdom.

## The Serious objections (9 items, 6 schools)
1. **Evil — hell and the Mary sentence.** The text admits grace kept Mary sinless without force, so "God does not force His love" cannot alone explain final loss; it strains SCG III.112, "never a mere means," "good that you exist, not for a while." Repair: concede hell as the heaviest weight; say the Church does not explain why God does not so win every will (grace and freedom unsettled).
2. **Evil — divine-command killings.** "He who gives life may call it home" is Aquinas's premise for killing at God's command; 1 Sam 15:3 turns the conclusion's test on the Amalekite infant. Repair: name it; *Dei Verbum* 15, *Verbum Domini* 42, Gen 18:25; the right is never ours (CCC 2258, 67).
3. **Debunker — W2's "reach" parity with logic fails Street's dilemma.** Logic helped because true; moral beliefs helped whether or not true. Reach begs the question against non-realists. Repair: aim reach at godless realism only; against the debunker rely on A4 (the belief evolution least predicts) and the fairness rule.
4. **Debunker — the God row claims theism "explains" guilt-toward-the-dead and the longing,** which W1(1)/W3 concede science explains too. Repair: "fits," "answers," not "explains"; they are clues.
5. **Aristotelian — the parasite.** "A person it never gives away … God does not give her to it" vs "so is theirs": host-specific human parasites exist. Repair: the body is corruptible within the order; the person is governed for her own sake (SCG III.112); creation became hostile when the first harmony was lost (CCC 376, 400, 1008).
6. **Buddhist — "What remains is a ripening."** False on the Buddhist account (the conventional person continues); inconsistent with crediting karmic justice "across lives"; omits cross-life reconciliation stories; "whether or not she pardons" is equally true of absolution. Repair: the real difference is that no one keeps her — the meeting is possible, not promised.
7. **Buddhist — "grounds regard in suffering"; God row's "rivals leave out why the line has no exceptions and is not hers to cross."** False against Theravāda (third pārājika: no killing at any number or at request). Repair: the difference is the ground (the killer's heart and her chance of freedom vs a worth she was given).
8. **Pragmatist — coverage.** W1 frame's "every account that keeps these words agrees: the wrong is wrong of necessity" is false of Rorty, Harman, Wong, Dewey; no C row; A heading and W2 note omit them. Repair: narrow the claim; add a row.
9. **Care ethics — coverage.** No row; the conclusion's choice (strength/mind vs "what she is") omits the relational answer (Kittay: "some mother's child"). Repair: add a row; conclusion adds "nor how much anyone cared for her, for no one did"; A5 names dependency (CCC 2208–2209).

## The pattern behind most findings
**Overclaiming in the comparison.** The God row and several "every"/"cannot explain"/"leave out" sentences claim more than the text's own W1(1), W3 and W1(2) concede (Debunker, Kantian, Buddhist, Contractualist, Existentialist, Spinozist, Realist). The book's own rule (CCC 31: converging, not compelling) forbids this. **Coverage.** Six schools found no row that fits them (pragmatist, care, existentialist, Marxist, Spinozist, Daoist). Each drafted one.
