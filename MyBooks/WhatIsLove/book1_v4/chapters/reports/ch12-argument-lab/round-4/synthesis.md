## The remaining Serious objections (three findings)
1. **Placement of the Church's teaching on chosen death and consent (Debunker, Realist, Utilitarian, Expressivist; Aristotelian and Contractualist as Minor).** The censor's correct Round 3 fixes ("never, if innocent, to be deliberately killed," with "the dying" in A4; "not even his own consent could make it right" under A6's "almost everyone sees") put a teaching many readers deny into premises offered as shared, and the God row then counted "not even her own to cross" as something rivals "leave out." **Fix (v5):** every word of the doctrine kept, moved under A6's own honest sentence ("those are this book's claims, argued in W1(2), not shared ones"); A4 limited to "deliberately killed for others' sake"; W1(2) offers "held in trust" as a reason *for those who hold the line at every number*, and the God row claims it only "for those who hold it with the Church." Key distinction added (utilitarian): "a man may lay down his life, but he may not hand it over to be destroyed."
2. **"Heaven first" (Evil).** v4 said love "must first be freely given before it can be confirmed," yet its own Dhamma row says God gives the highest good to infants (CCC 1250). **Fix:** a good, not a necessity; hell's finality rests on the Church's teaching (CCC 393, 1033, 1857), "taught, not shown."
3. **"No account that counts only totals can say why the line is there" (Utilitarian).** Hare and rule consequentialism do say why. **Fix:** they can say why holding it is good; not why crossing it wrongs *her*.
