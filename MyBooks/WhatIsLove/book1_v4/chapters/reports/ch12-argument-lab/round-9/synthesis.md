## Patrick's decision (binding for v9)
**1 Samuel 15:3: hold with St. Thomas.** The Author of life is not unjust in calling any life — even all existence — home; one who obeys such a command acts only as His instrument, so the right is never ours (ST I-II q.94 a.5 ad 2; II-II q.64 a.6 ad 1; I-II q.100 a.8 ad 3). Name Thomas; do not set the Church against him (DV 11, 15; VD 42). Own the cost against Kant openly. Patrick also asks: if the atheist feels such indignation at injustice, why? — put this as an invitation in the evil section (his protest stands on A1–A4, the ground this case starts from; follow it to its source), never as a jab.

## The Error (censor)
Kant row: "the one forgiven must still … make satisfaction" and "in both halves" are false for Baptism, which remits all sin and all punishment (CCC 1263; Council of Florence; ST III q.68 a.5), and "both halves" implies God does not forgive sins between persons. Fix: restitution stays; satisfaction only for sins after Baptism; drop "both halves."

## The nine Serious items
1. **Care (new row):** "stops at the infant no one holds" is a false dilemma; a standing tie makes care owed even when refused (Tronto: society responsible). Real stopping point = why that tie gives equal standing (as the contractualist row says).
2. **Pragmatist (new row):** "cannot say why 'we' should widen" is false (Rorty: cruelty is the worst thing we do). Real price: nothing beyond our continuing practice keeps her claim true. James's test (*Pragmatism* III).
3. **Debunker (A4/W2, added in v8):** "the belief survival least predicts" — no debunker is instinct-only; cultural evolution + consistency predicts the late, contested history; theism predicts it too. A4's weight is the reader's direct certainty. (Round 8's debunker had suggested this line; Round 9's rejects it.)
4. **Evil (evil section "Second"):** "not a trade; keep why and what apart" collides with CCC 412 ("permits evil in order to draw forth some greater good") and with skeptical theism; infants who die before choosing gain nothing from their own freedom and can receive their good as gift (CCC 1250) — this sharpens Rowe. Repair honestly: CCC 412 says He permits for a greater good; the book's claim is narrower (no one is spent as a mere means; the good is offered to her); concede the infant case as weight.
5. **Evil (1 Sam 15):** settled by Patrick's decision above; make the text consistent with it.
6. **Realist (W2 reach):** survival + consistency is the realist's explanation; reduce to a parity question (how minds grasp necessary truths at all) plus unity.
7. **Realist (worth by kind):** the realist grounds it in the kind's essence just as the text grounds it in "what she is"; ST I q.20 a.3 and HG 26 trap. Narrower edge: why there is such a kind at all, and what it is for (creation is the hinge).
8. **Nietzschean (Power row):** "resentment does not explain regard for every human being" misreads him (A 20–22; GM I.7, I.14); the "same Christian ancestry" fallback doesn't cut both ways. Fix: a belief's history defeats A4 only by assuming there is no God; equal worth was also held outside Christianity (Zhu Xi, buddha-nature). TI "Skirmishes" 5: equal worth stands or falls with God.
9. **Daoist (new row):** "Nor does God possess" vs CCC 2280's "sovereign Master" — the text claims agreement where they part ("rears and rules" vs "rears and does not rule").

## The lesson for v9
New text is where the new faults are. Each new row overclaimed against the school it describes. **v9 should narrow rather than add**: test every sentence that characterizes a rival by asking whether that school's best defender would sign the description; replace "cannot"/"stops at"/"leaves out" with the real difference; prefer cutting a claim to defending it. Net length should not grow.
