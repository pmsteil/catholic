## What the author's side holds to
- Catholic teaching is the frame. Reason's witness is "converging and convincing" (CCC 31) and "can predispose one to faith" (CCC 35); it does not replace faith. Overclaiming is a fault.
- God's authority is the Creator's: to create, to establish what we are for, and to define what love must answer to, because He is the Good (no voluntarism).
- Unbelievers are not presumed culpable (CCC 847, LG 16).
- Human dignity is "fully recognizable even by reason alone" (Dignitas Infinita 1); the question is whether a non-theist can GROUND equal, unconditional dignity.
- Premises a non-Christian must grant may not contain Christian-only demands.
