# Blind review: the Marxist school

## 1. School and voice

I speak for historical materialism and its moral heirs: Marx and Engels on morality as answering to the mode of production and religion as the protest and consolation of the oppressed; Allen Wood's reading that Marx condemned capitalism for unfreedom, domination and stunted self-realization rather than for injustice; Norman Geras's and Ziyad Husami's reply that Marx's language of robbery shows he thought it unjust anyway; Steven Lukes's diagnosis that Marxism's contempt for *Recht* left it defenseless against means-and-ends reasoning; G. A. Cohen's analytic egalitarianism; Terry Eagleton's bodily, Aristotelian Marxism. My strongest weapon is the critique of ideology. Under the fairness rule it may not serve as a refutation by origins, because Marxism has an ancestry too (Löwith's "secularized eschatology," verify), and the text could turn that on me. So I use it to test *explanatory* claims the text makes, and the *function* of its distinctions, never to say "class-born, therefore false."

## 2. Answers to the four standing challenges

1. **Starting point for justice.** Wood's Marx: there is none outside history. Justice is the fit of transactions to a mode of production (*Capital* III, ch. 21, paraphrase, verify). We condemn exploitation and domination by real human goods (freedom, community, many-sided self-realization), not by what anyone is "owed." Cohen: a fundamental, fact-insensitive principle of equality plus community. **Verdict:** Wood's version holds on its own terms but **breaks** at "owed," and cannot say the exploited worker was *wronged*. Cohen's version **borrows**: it is godless realism and meets W2's question.
2. **A hard dilemma.** A union steward learns that the only plant in a poor town is unsafe. Disclosure will close it and cost 3,000 jobs; silence risks a Bhopal. My principles: those who bear a risk decide it (democratic control of production); tell the workers and the town the truth, not management alone; fight for both safety and jobs, by conversion, public ownership or strike; and refuse the frame of a forced choice, because capital's ownership of the plant manufactures that frame. **Verdict:** coherent as a method, but it **borrows** truthfulness (A7) and the rule that one may not gamble other people's lives without their say (close to A4/A6). "Solidarity" does the work of self-gift, and I ground it in species-being, not in a gift of covenant.
3. **Equal, unconditional dignity.** Marx refused "equal right" because it measures unequal individuals by one standard taken from one side only (*Critique of the Gotha Programme*, paraphrase, verify). Instead: to each according to need, which gives most to those who can produce least. Eagleton grounds care in the needy, vulnerable human body. **Verdict:** **holds** for an unconditional claim to have needs met. **Breaks** at *equal* worth and at *prior to anyone's recognition*. The Marxist end, free conscious activity, is one the newborn who will never think cannot reach, and need claims are met "when abundance allows," not owed under scarcity.
4. **Ending point.** The free development of each as the condition of the free development of all; a realm of freedom beyond necessity; the reconciliation of humanity with nature and with itself. Guiding principles: abolish exploitation and domination, meet needs, practice solidarity, and test theory by practice. **Verdict:** **holds** for the living who can act. **Breaks** for the dying, for those who never will act, and above all for the dead. Horkheimer conceded that past injustice is done with and the slain are really slain (letter to Benjamin, 1937, paraphrase, verify).

## 3. Debate highlights

**E1. The starting point is ideology.** *Attack:* A offers its items "as truths," not as one community's best words. Engels held that all morality so far has been class morality, and that no moral truth is eternal (*Anti-Dühring* I.9, paraphrase, verify). What "still seems true to us after honest reflection" was formed in a class society, so the reflecting "we" is located, not neutral. *Defense:* A's own rule already admits the Marxist defeater: a belief loses its warrant when it is shown that "what produced the belief had nothing to do with its truth." That has to be shown item by item, and for A1 it cannot be. Engels himself affirmed moral progress and a coming "really human morality" beyond class (paraphrase, verify). Marx's indictment of child labor in *Capital* I, ch. 10, presupposes A1. If class origin voided A1, it would void Marx's own indictment. *Rejoinder:* granted. Ideology critique is targeted, not global, and A's rule gives it exactly that role. **Held.**

**E2. A4 is not "least predicted": Marx predicted it.** *Attack:* the text rests its reply to the debunker on A4, "the belief survival least predicts." But the strongest naturalist account of A4 is historical, not Darwinian. Marx argued that Aristotle could not decipher the equality hidden in exchange-value because Greek society rested on slavery. Human equality becomes a fixed popular conviction only where the commodity form is universal (*Capital* I, ch. 1 §3, paraphrase, verify). Pashukanis derived the equal legal subject from the exchanger. On this account A4 is just what a world with no one behind it predicts, once you add history to survival. *Defense:* the sentence is restricted to accounts "built only for survival," so as written it is true. More to the point, commodity equality is the equality of those who own something to sell. It predicts the workhouse, Malthus, the "relative surplus population" and the infant as a cost. It does not predict worth for the one who can neither sell nor struggle. A4's distinctive content (the newborn, the dying, the severely disabled, "where instinct fails") is the part market equality predicts *least*. *Rejoinder:* the extension came through struggle: labor movements, mothers, disability-rights campaigns. *Reply:* struggle explains why the claim was heard, not why it was true, and the infant on the hillside cannot struggle. **Held with repair.** The text should name the historical-materialist rival and answer it as above (Repair R7).

**E3. "Stunted but not wronged" is a false dilemma.** *Attack:* the C row offers two horns: justice relative to each age, so the slave was "stunted but not wronged," or transhistorical principles, which meet W2. Wood's Marx is neither. He condemns slavery and wage labor as domination and exploitation, and those are *relational* evils, done by someone to someone, not mere stunting. Marx called Spartacus the finest figure in ancient history and a true representative of the ancient proletariat (letter to Engels, 27 Feb 1861, paraphrase, verify). *Defense:* "stunted" is a misstatement and should be fixed. The substance survives. Domination condemned without "owed" still cannot say what she was owed, and that is A2's whole point: *I did this to her*, and she can forgive it. Geras's reading, that Marx thought capitalism unjust without thinking he thought so (paraphrase, verify), simply takes the second horn. Marx's sympathy for Spartacus is a case of sympathies outrunning theory. Using it this way is no genealogy; it is the same move the text makes, fairly, with Confucius. *Rejoinder:* Cohen takes the second horn openly and says fundamental principles hold without a ground. That is godless realism, a standoff at W2. **Held with repair** (R1).

**E4. Double effect is clean hands for capital.** *Attack:* A6 says "side effects may be weighed; persons may not be spent." Engels called it "social murder" when a society knowingly keeps workers in conditions that kill them early, murder disguised because no one seems to do it (*Condition of the Working Class*, 1845, paraphrase, verify). The intention/foresight line makes the market's killings weighable, while the private acts of the weak (the dying man's request, the desperate) fall under absolutes. A morality whose absolutes reach the poor and whose weighing reaches the rich is ideology in the strict sense. *Defense:* the text has already closed most of this. A6 says weighing is no license, and that usurious and avaricious dealings that lead to hunger and death indirectly commit homicide (CCC 2269), which is the Church's own "social murder." A2 gives the structures of sin (CCC 1868–1869). Decisively, the Church lists among the *intrinsically evil* acts, which no intention or circumstance can make right, "degrading conditions of work which treat labourers as mere instruments of profit" (*Veritatis Splendor* 80, quoting *Gaudium et Spes* 27). The absolute reaches the employer. *Rejoinder:* how the Church applied this in practice is history. Under fairness I may not use it unless I let the text use the Soviet labor camps. **Held.** It would be stronger still if VS 80 were named (R5).

**E5. The hope is the opium.** *Attack:* religion is the sigh of the oppressed creature: real protest, false fulfillment (Marx, 1844, paraphrase, verify). W1(1)'s kept dead, W3's longing and the evil section's resurrection console where they should mobilize. The demand to give up illusions about one's condition is the demand to give up a condition that needs illusions. *Defense:* as a refutation this is a genealogy, and the text can return it. The Marxist longing for history's riddle to be solved and humanity reconciled has its own messianic ancestry. Neither ancestry settles anything ("A history settles neither"). On the merits, A7 already forbids believing because a belief comforts, and the text grades W1(1), W3 and the resurrection hope as clues, "not evidence." Against quietism the text says His permission "never excuses ours" (CCC 2269). And the Marxist tradition's own deepest thinker on the dead, Benjamin, held that the past has a claim on us that remembrance must answer, and granted in his notes that this was theology (*Arcades* N8,1; Theses II, paraphrase, verify). Horkheimer answered him that the slain are really slain. The text's line, "to the children already spent they can offer memory, not repair," is exactly Horkheimer's honesty. *Rejoinder:* conceded as a refutation. One residue stands: W1(1) weighs only as far as one holds that a debt truly owed must be receivable, and I deny that. A debt to the irrecoverable can be honored by remembrance and by struggle in her name. The text names this exact standoff. **Held** (standoff at parity, honestly labelled).

**E6. "Equal" is bourgeois right.** *Attack:* the text grounds equal worth by measuring every person "against one end that is God Himself." That is Marx's target in the Gotha critique: to make people equal you must bring them under one standard and see them from one side only. Need is better than equal worth, because it meets each person in her particularity and asks most for those who can do least. *Defense:* the one end is not one side of a person. It is the completion of the whole person, and it is given, not achieved (CCC 1261, 1023). It is also differentiated: the saints are equal in worth and unequal in how well they love (A4). That is equality of worth together with irreducible particularity, which is what Marx wanted "beyond right." Need, by contrast, says what is to be met, not what is owed under scarcity, and the Marxist end of free, conscious, many-sided activity is one the newborn who will never think can never reach. The text's end can be given to her. *Rejoinder:* none at parity. Eagleton's needy body grounds care, not *equal* worth. **Held** (for the text). On challenge 3 my school **breaks**.

**E7. "Lord of life" mirrors earthly lordship.** *Attack:* W1(2) makes life "held in trust" from a "sovereign Master." Feuerbach and Marx read heavenly sovereignty as earthly domination projected upward. A Master over persons is the slaveholder's model. *Defense:* the text's Master is the reverse of an owner. He "willed her for her own sake (GS 24)," gave her life "to her keeping," and "never spends her as a mere means." Projection as an argument is again a genealogy. The analytic Marxist tradition has a stake here too. Cohen found that the classic exploitation charge, that the worker is robbed of what he makes, leans on the worker's owning himself, which is Nozick's premise, and Cohen came to reject self-ownership (*Self-Ownership, Freedom and Equality*, 1995, paraphrase, verify). The trust model, together with the universal destination of goods (CCC 2402–2404), grounds the worker's claim without self-ownership. *Rejoinder:* Cohen's own way out was equality as a fundamental principle, so this is a standoff at W2, but the insight is real. **Held.**

**E8. The engine of moral correction is struggle, not consistency.** *Attack:* W2 says conscience corrects itself, and "the engine of that correction is reason holding itself to consistency." Seneca had the consistency argument; it moved nothing for eighteen centuries. What moved things was the resistance of the dominated (Haiti, the abolitionists who had been slaves, the labor movement) under changed material conditions. Practice, not reflection, settled the question (Thesis II on Feuerbach, paraphrase, verify). *Defense:* the wronged won by making claims that were true and could be recognized as true. "Am I not a man and a brother?" is an appeal to consistency, and the Church speaks of the cry of the poor that must be heard. *Rejoinder:* then say both. **Held with repair** (R6). This is Minor: the witness of W2 survives if the wording admits that the push usually came from the wronged.

## 5. Remaining sound objections against the text AS WRITTEN

**As written: NONE.**

**Minor:**
- W2 credits moral self-correction to "reason holding itself to consistency" alone. Historically the push came mostly from the wronged and their struggles (E8).
- A4/W2 answer only the Darwinian rival to A4. The historical-materialist account of how human equality arose (the commodity form; Marx on Aristotle) is the stronger naturalist rival, and the text can beat it, but should say so (E2).
- A2's grammar of guilt handles structural harm only through culpable "many hands." This does not touch W1, but a Marxist reader will notice it.
- A6 answers Engels' "social murder" with CCC 2269 but leaves out the stronger text, VS 80 / GS 27.

**Misstatements of my school (C: History and class):**
- "the slave of antiquity was stunted but not wronged": on Wood's reading Marx condemns slavery as *domination and exploitation*, which are relational evils, not mere stunting. The text's point survives with the right word.
- "Need reaches the newborn but does not make her worth as much as you": true in effect, but Marx's reason is that he rejected measuring persons by any one standard at all (Gotha). The row should say that, because it is where Marx is both strongest and weakest.
- "its humane heirs restored the line": they restored constraints against spending the innocent, but few Marxists hold that line at every number.

**Honest standoffs at parity (not defects):**
- Cohen-style fact-insensitive principles versus the text's "held first in a Mind" (W2), the same standoff as with godless realism.
- Whether a debt to the dead must be receivable (W1(1)): Horkheimer says no and remembrance suffices; the text says it weighs only as much as the reader holds otherwise. The text labels this exactly.
- Ideology critique against Christian practice versus the Christian critique of Marxist practice. Under fairness, both histories cancel.
- Practice (Thesis II) versus reflection as the test of moral truth.

## 6. Proposed repairs

**R1 (C, History and class).**
Old: "If justice is only what fits each age, the slave of antiquity was stunted but not wronged (A1, A2);"
New: "If justice is only what fits each age, the slave of antiquity was dominated and exploited, and Marx took Spartacus's side, but she was not wronged in any sense that says what she was owed (A1, A2); that condemnation rests on freedom and flourishing, and joins the flourishing of our kind;"

## 7. New insights for the book; lines worth keeping

**Insights:**
- **VS 80 / GS 27** is the Church's answer to Marx in Marx's own register: treating workers as mere instruments of profit is an intrinsic evil, not a weighable side effect. It turns the clean-hands charge around. Use it in A6 and in the Marxist row.
- **The commodity-form rival to A4.** The best naturalist explanation of equal worth is Marx's, not Darwin's, and it explains the equality of exchangers, not of the infant. Answering it makes A4 stronger than answering survival alone does.
- **Benjamin and Horkheimer** are the Marxist tradition's own debate about the dead. Horkheimer's "the slain are really slain" is the honest Marxist position the text already describes. Benjamin's turn to theology is the strain, to be used exactly as the text uses Confucius: as strain, not failure.
- **Cohen on self-ownership.** The Marxist exploitation charge needs a ground other than self-ownership, and the Catholic trust / universal-destination model offers one. This is a real point of convergence the book can claim.
- **Struggle and consistency.** Admitting that the wronged pushed moral correction does not weaken W2. It fits the Church's language of the cry of the poor.
- **Work as a basic good** (*Laborem Exercens* 9) is the one good a Marxist will find missing from A5.

**Lines worth keeping:**
- "A history settles neither."
- "Side effects may be weighed; persons may not be spent."
- "Weighing is not a license."
- "the Marxist works for a world in which no one is spent for another's gain"
- "to the children already spent they can offer memory, not repair"
- "His permission never excuses ours"
- "God never spends anyone as a mere means against her own good."
- "The rebel can take her side; he cannot keep her."
- "What rises in you then is not the source of her worth. It is your answer to it."

## 8. Score

**8/10.** The text anticipates the Marxist critique unusually well (structures of sin, CCC 2269, Adorno via *Spe Salvi*, a fair dilemma on justice), applies the fairness rule to both sides, and leaves my school breaking at "owed," at *equal* worth and at the dead. What it lacks is a sharper statement of Wood's Marx, the VS 80 answer to "social murder," and a reply to the commodity-form account of equality. **Honest ceiling** for any theistic case against Marxism: about **8.5**. It can show that Marxism cannot ground "owed," equal worth or repair for the dead, but a Cohen-style Marxist who takes equality as a brute, fact-insensitive principle and accepts Horkheimer's verdict on the dead stands at parity, and reason alone cannot move him past that.
