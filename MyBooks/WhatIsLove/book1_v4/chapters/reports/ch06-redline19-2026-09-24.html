The Church, the living witness

line 259

… As Scott Hahn has put it, the New Testament was a sacrament before it was a document. The covenant was Christ Himself, given in the Eucharist and lived out by a people drawn into His love; the inspired books are the written witness to that covenant, not its beginning; the Church is its living witness. Before there was ever a New Testament on a shelf, there was a Church bringing people into the love of Christ , sent by Christ to bear witness to Him to the end of the earth (cf. Acts 1:8), bringing people into His love.
