Father and mother: moved to Chapter 11

Section: Unity Requires Submission to the Authority of His Perfect Love · line 302

Many families know the pattern behind these examples: a father who leans toward justice, a mother who leans toward mercy. This is part of how God made us. The Church teaches that the "difference and complementarity" of man and woman "are oriented toward the goods of marriage and the flourishing of family life" (CCC 2333). Each needs the other. His justice without her mercy crushes a child; her mercy without his justice spoils one. Held together, they show a child what God's love looks like, for in our Father in heaven, justice and mercy are one.
