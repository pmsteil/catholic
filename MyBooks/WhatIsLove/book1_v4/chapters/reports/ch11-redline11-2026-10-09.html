Your addition, in the Church's words. Giving one's life for another with good reason stays the summit of love (the sentence still says “even our lives”); what the Church forbids is treating life as ours to throw away. CCC 2280: “We are stewards, not owners, of the life God has entrusted to us” (vatican.va). The Catechism applies the same principle to reckless risk-taking in CCC 2290. “Carelessly” carries your “flippantly” in plainer words.

Redline 11 · our own soul, and our own life

Section: The Order of Love · line 116

… Our own soul — never to be sacrificed. We give our comfort, our time, our preferences, even our lives — but we may never destroy the image of God within us by choosing sin to "help" someone else. Nor may we treat our own life as cheap, putting ourselves in harm's way carelessly or without a just reason: "We are stewards, not owners, of the life God has entrusted to us" (CCC 2280).
