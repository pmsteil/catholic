line 214

When we voluntarily submit to God's authority—grounded in truth and expressed through love—we become slaves to Love itself. And being enslaved to perfect Love place ourselves under Him as a willing servant places himself under his master. And then He does what no earthly master does: He makes His servants sons and daughters. "So through God you are no longer a slave but a son, and if a son then an heir" (Galatians 4:7). The Christian, the Church says, "no longer stands before God as a slave, in servile fear, or as a mercenary looking for wages, but as a son responding to the love of him who 'first loved us'" (CCC 1828). That is perfect freedom, because Love wills our good, knows our nature, and leads us to our true fulfillment. This is why the saints, who were most "enslaved" gave themselves most completely to God, were also the freest people who ever lived.
